# Privacy

- **Parrot sends nothing itself.** The server runs on your Mac, reads Parrot's local meetings and answers Claude's tool calls. It makes no network requests.
- **Claude receives what the tools return.** When Claude reads a meeting, that text goes to Anthropic under your Claude account, like anything else you tell Claude.
- **You choose what Claude can see**, in Parrot → Claude & AI Apps: transcripts, reports and notes (on by default), Copilot cards (off by default), and call types to hide. Meetings marked on-device only, audio, API keys and settings are never shown.
- **Read-only.** No tool changes, deletes or records anything. The only file it writes is a copy of a meeting you ask to export, in Downloads/Parrot Exports.
- **Off until you turn it on**, and turning it off cuts Claude off on its next request.
- **Parrot keeps counts, not content**: how many times an AI app read your meetings today and when, so the Claude & AI Apps page can show it.

Privacy policy: [openparrot.app/privacy](https://openparrot.app/privacy). Every detail: [What leaves your Mac](https://openparrot.app/help/privacy.html).
